In brief

This is a free kit for people who sell to businesses and want to prospect without blasting cold email lists. The idea is to look for signals—public events that suggest a company may need your help now, such as a job opening, a new product launch, or a new executive—and only then find the decision-maker and write a message that mentions that signal. Claude Code handles the heavy lifting with Clay (a platform for company and contact data) and delivers a spreadsheet for you to review. You need Claude Code, a Clay account (with a free plan and trial), and an afternoon to fill in your business context.

The problem and the idea

Cold lists wear everyone out. Signals start conversations.

Traditional prospecting buys a list, sends everyone the same message, and hopes for the best. Almost nobody replies, your domain lands in spam, and your brand gets a reputation for being annoying.

🧊 The problem

Cold lists with no context, generic AI-written messages, dozens of tabs and tools, and emails that go straight to spam.

📡 The idea

Prospect based on signals: first find out who has a reason to talk now, then find the right person and bring up that reason.

🧠 How the work is split

Clay handles the data (companies, job openings, emails). Claude Code handles the tools: you ask in plain language, and it does the work. You review and decide.

How it works

The 7 steps

Each step filters the previous one. Costly lookups (people, emails) are only run for companies that pass the signal check.

1 · ICP→ 2 · Hiring signal→ 3 · Research agent→ 4 · Decision-maker→ 5 · Waterfall→ 6 · Message→ 7 · Review + send
1

ICP: the company list

ICP means ideal customer profile. You set the industry, company size, and country; Clay returns matching companies.

2

Hiring signal

Clay tracks open roles that matter to you. An AI job opening, for example, may point to a new AI project.

3

Research agent

An AI agent column reads websites, blogs, and news, then answers with a link: has the company launched a new product in recent months?

4

Find decision-makers only where there is a signal

The people search has an execution condition: it only runs for companies with a signal. This is where you save credits.

5

Waterfall enrichment

Multiple email providers run in sequence; the waterfall stops at the first valid result. Then the email is verified.

6

Contextual message

Claude Code reads your business files and writes a subject line and body that mention the company’s signal, with a simple question and an easy way to opt out.

7

Human review + sequencer

You review the spreadsheet, then import it into your sequencer and send from a warmed-up domain with a low daily send volume.

Step-by-step user guide

From zero to a reviewed spreadsheet

Commands starting with / are typed inside Claude Code. Enter the others in the terminal.

1

Download the kit and open Claude Code in the folder 🧠 you

The kit becomes your prospecting project folder. Its CLAUDE.md tells Claude Code how to behave (ask before spending credits, run 10 rows first, never send anything).

git clone https://github.com/inematds/prospeccao-por-sinais.git
cp -r prospeccao-por-sinais/kit ~/minha-prospeccao
cd ~/minha-prospeccao && claude
2

Install the official Clay plugin 🧠 you

In Claude Code, add the marketplace and install the plugin. Then ask it to run the setup skill, which installs the clay command-line tool and signs you in.

/plugin marketplace add clay-run/agent-plugins
/plugin install clay@clay-plugins
# then, in English:
Run the clay:setup skill and sign me in to Clay.

Using Codex? The equivalent commands are codex plugin marketplace add clay-run/agent-plugins and installing clay under Plugins.

3

Fill in your business context 🧠 you

Open the 6 templates in contexto/ (profile, offer, cases, proof, FAQ, and voice). Use contexto/exemplo/ (a fictional company) as a reference. The agent must not make claims that are not in provas.md.

# ask Claude Code for help
Read contexto/exemplo/ and interview me to fill in contexto/ with my company’s information.
4

Define your signals 🧠 you + 🤖 agent

Edit sinais.yaml: each signal has a validity window in days and a weight. To get help finding the signals that matter in your market, try this public signal-generation skill:

# optional, in the terminal
npx skills add clay-run/clay-skill-creator --skill customer-signal-generator
5

Test with 10 rows 🤖 agent · ⚙️ Clay

Before any larger batch, run steps 1 and 2 of the workflow on 10 companies. Check how many have a signal and how many have people with verified emails. Prompts for each step are in prompts/passo-a-passo.md (in Portuguese).

Follow Step 1 and Step 2 in prompts/passo-a-passo.md for 10 companies.
Show me the results and estimated cost before running more.
6

Run /goal with a clear finish line 🤖 agent · ⚙️ Clay

/goal tells Claude Code to keep going until a verifiable condition is met: N leads, no blank columns, a verified email, a subject and body that mention the signal, a low-pressure call to action, and a final CSV. The full prompt is in prompts/goal-prospeccao.md (in Portuguese).

/goal I want 25 new signal-based prospecting leads, ready for human review.
... (copy the rest of prompts/goal-prospeccao.md)

Even within /goal, CLAUDE.md tells the agent to stop and ask for your approval before each step that spends credits.

7

Review the spreadsheet 🧠 you

Open saida/leads-AAAA-MM-DD.csv. The columns are listed in colunas-csv.md (in Portuguese). Start with rows marked revisar = sim, read each message, and check the signal link.

# main columns
empresa, decisor, cargo, email, email_status, sinal, resumo_sinal,
link_fonte_sinal, gancho, assunto, corpo, revisar
8

Import into your sequencer and send slowly 🧠 you · ⚙️ sequencer

In Clay: import data, CSV, new table, then create a campaign using that table as the list. Send from a warmed-up secondary domain, with a low daily send volume, and increase it gradually. Anyone who asks to opt out goes into supressao.csv (in Portuguese).

# rule of thumb
warmed-up secondary domain → low daily send volume → increase gradually
spam rate below 0.3% (Gmail sender requirement)

Brazil

Using this in Brazil: LGPD and data coverage

The workflow works here, with two things to keep in mind.

⚖️ LGPD

Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD) is often the legal basis for B2B prospecting when it involves legitimate interest. In practice:

  • the contact is relevant to the person’s role;
  • clearly identify who is writing;
  • make it easy to opt out of every message;
  • always respect the suppression list;
  • don’t use sensitive personal data;
  • keep the source of each data point.

The kit includes checklist-lgpd.md (in Portuguese). This isn’t legal advice; check with your legal team. References: Brazilian Law 13,709/2018 and the ANPD (Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority).

🗺️ Data coverage varies by country

Data providers vary in their coverage across countries and industries. So:

  • always test 10 rows and see how many return with a verified email;
  • if coverage is weak, ask the agent to search the company website (contact page, team page);
  • for a small local business (clinic, shop, office), use Google Maps and the website to build your list.
Common mistakes

What goes wrong (and how to avoid it)

Almost every problem comes from skipping one of the CLAUDE.md rules.

✗ Run the whole table right away

It can use up the month’s credits on a bad setup. Always start with 10 rows.

✗ Search for decision-makers at every company

This is the biggest waste. Use the execution condition: search only where there is a signal.

✗ Empty context

Without a profile, cases, and proof, AI writes a generic message. Fill in contexto/ first.

✗ A signal with no source or past its date

Mentioning a job opening that has closed sounds careless. Require a link and respect each signal’s time window.

✗ Send from your main domain

If it lands in spam, your company’s email reputation suffers too. Use a warmed-up secondary domain.

✗ High volume on day one

Send a few emails per day, increase gradually, and keep the spam rate below 0.3%.
